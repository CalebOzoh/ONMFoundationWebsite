Donate

Why your support matters.

A gift to the Foundation funds preventive healthcare, hygiene kits, digital training and school outreach for women and girls who would otherwise go without. Every naira is directed toward one of our active programmes.

Secure Giving

Give a one-time or recurring gift

Donations are processed securely via Selar. You will receive an email confirmation immediately after giving.

Transparency

Where your gift goes.

Illustrative allocation of a typical gift across our current programme mix. Actual allocation may vary by campaign and is reviewed by our board each cycle.

AllocationWhere it goes
70%Direct programme delivery, including training, health outreach, hygiene kits and school programmes
15%Community & conference operations
10%Monitoring, evaluation & reporting
5%Administration

Formal audited financial statements are available to institutional donors and partners on request. Please write to admin@onmfoundation.org.
