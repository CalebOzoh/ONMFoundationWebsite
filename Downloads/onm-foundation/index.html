Who We Are

A foundation built on one woman's legacy, carried forward for every woman after her.

The Obiianuju-Nnenna Media Foundation exists to help women live healthier, lead with confidence and build lives of substance, regardless of where they start.

We work at the intersection of health, digital skills, economic empowerment and leadership, because a resilient woman builds a resilient household, and a resilient household builds a resilient community. Our approach is deliberately practical. We meet women where they are, in schools, markets, places of worship and online, then we walk with them from awareness to action.
