Who We Are

A foundation built on one woman's legacy, carried forward for every woman after her.

The Obiianuju-Nnenna Media (ONM) Foundation is a women-led, impact-driven non-governmental organisation committed to improving the lives of women, girls, young people and underserved communities.

We believe that when women and girls are healthy, educated, confident, financially empowered and supported, they become catalysts for stronger families, thriving communities and sustainable social transformation. Our approach is deliberately practical. We meet women where they are, in schools, markets, places of worship and online, then we walk with them from awareness to action.
