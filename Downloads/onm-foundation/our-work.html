Our Work

Six pillars. Eight initiatives. One connected ecosystem.

In alignment with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals 3, 4, 5, 8 and 17, we design interconnected programmes that improve lives, strengthen families and build resilient communities.

Six Strategic Pillars

Key initiatives and strategic objectives

Our work is anchored on six strategic pillars. Each one addresses a different barrier women and girls face, and together they form a single system of support rather than a set of isolated projects.

01
Health & Wellbeing

Healthier lives, before crisis arrives.

Promoting healthier lives through preventive healthcare, women's and menstrual health, mental and emotional wellbeing, nutrition, fitness and healthy lifestyle education. We advance health awareness, early detection and access to essential health information and services.

Preventive HealthcareMenstrual HealthMental WellbeingNutritionFitnessEarly Detection
02
Education & Digital Inclusion

Closing the learning and digital divide.

Expanding access to education, digital literacy, technology and future-ready skills for women, girls and young people. We work to bridge learning and digital divides while creating opportunities for lifelong learning, innovation and participation in the digital economy.

Digital LiteracyEducation AccessFuture Ready SkillsInnovationDigital Economy
03
Leadership & Personal Development

Confidence is a skill. We teach it.

Equipping girls and women with the confidence, knowledge and skills to become purposeful, resilient and values-driven leaders through mentorship, leadership development, capacity building and personal growth opportunities.

MentorshipLeadership DevelopmentCapacity BuildingPersonal Growth
04
Economic Empowerment & Financial Independence

Building the boss, not just the business.

Strengthening women's financial confidence and independence through entrepreneurship support, financial literacy, business development, digital skills and opportunities for sustainable livelihoods and wealth creation.

EntrepreneurshipFinancial LiteracyBusiness DevelopmentWealth Creation
05
Community Development, Protection & Support

Places to connect, heal, learn and thrive.

Building inclusive and supportive communities where women and girls can connect, heal, learn and thrive. Our work includes mentorship, peer support, community engagement, protection initiatives and support for vulnerable populations, including women affected by gender-based violence.

Peer SupportCommunity EngagementProtectionGBV Support
06
Advocacy, Media & Storytelling

Amplifying the voices that change norms.

Amplifying women's voices and experiences through media, strategic communications, storytelling and advocacy. We work to challenge harmful social norms, promote gender equality, increase visibility and inspire action towards positive social change.

MediaStrategic CommunicationsStorytellingGender Equality
The ONM Ecosystem

Our Programmes & Platforms

Our strategic pillars are brought to life through an integrated ecosystem of eight initiatives.

1

The Resilient Woman Africa

Our flagship women's empowerment platform promoting wholeness, wellness, leadership and financial confidence through the Resilient Woman Conference, Network and Podcast.

Explore the platform
2

RIA Magazine Africa

Our storytelling and advocacy platform amplifying African women's voices, experiences, achievements and opportunities through inspiring stories and practical knowledge.

Read RIA Magazine
3

Wellness Chronicles

Our preventive health initiative promoting health awareness, early detection, healthy lifestyles and access to essential health information and services.

4

MenaRise Initiative

Our menstrual health and dignity initiative advancing education, sustainable menstrual product access, school outreach and advocacy to address period poverty and break menstrual stigma.

5

HERLead

A leadership and mentorship programme equipping girls and young women aged 16 and above with the confidence, knowledge and skills to become purposeful, independent and influential leaders.

6

Digitiva

Our digital education and empowerment initiative equipping women and girls with practical digital skills, technological knowledge and entrepreneurial capabilities to thrive in the digital economy.

7

Friends of ONM Foundation

A community of donors, volunteers, partners, ambassadors and advocates committed to sustaining our mission through giving, collaboration and active participation.

Become a Friend
8

ONM Centre for Health, Healing & Empowerment Proposed

Our proposed integrated facility bringing together healthcare, fitness, therapy, skills development and safe accommodation to support healing, recovery, wellbeing and independence, particularly for women and vulnerable populations.

Together, these initiatives create a connected support system through which women and girls can access opportunities to learn, grow, lead, become economically independent and build healthier, more fulfilling lives.

Where We Started

Earlier programmes

These earlier programmes shaped the ecosystem we run today. Several have since grown into the named initiatives above, and the work they began continues under those banners.

BossLady Business & Finance Bootcamp

Entrepreneurship, financial literacy and wealth building, now carried forward under Economic Empowerment.

Send A Girl Back To School

Girls' education, school outreach and mentorship, now carried forward under HERLead and Education & Digital Inclusion.

ThrivePad and the Period Dignity Project

Menstrual health education and hygiene kit distribution, now carried forward under the MenaRise Initiative.

Female Alive Campaign

Preventive health awareness for women, now carried forward under Wellness Chronicles.

NextGen Women Leaders Programme

Mentorship, confidence building and public speaking, now carried forward under HERLead.

ONM Wellness Centre

Our original wellness facility concept, now developed into the ONM Centre for Health, Healing & Empowerment.

Building women. Strengthening communities. Creating sustainable impact across Nigeria and beyond.
